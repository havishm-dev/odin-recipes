Peanut Butter and Jelly Bread Pudding

Description

Peanut Butter and Jelly Bread Pudding is a comforting dessert made with soft bread, creamy peanut butter, and swett jelly. It is a simple way to turn everyday ingredients into a warm and satisfying dessert.

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Peanut Butter
  • Jelly
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat the over
  2. Spread peanut butter and jelly onto the bread
  3. Cut the bread into pieces and place them in a baking dish
  4. Whisk together the eggs, milk, sugar, and vanilla
  5. Pour the mixture over the bread and let it soak
  6. Bake until the pudding is golden and set
  7. Let it cool slightly and serve
