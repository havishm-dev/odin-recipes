Garlic Butter Pasta

Description

Garlic butter pasta is a quick and comforting dish made with pasta tossed in melted butter, garlic, and simple seasonings. It is an easy meal that can be prepared with just a few ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Parmesan Cheese
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Parsley
  • Olive Oil

Steps

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil
  2. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions
  3. While the pasta cooks, melt butter in a pan over medium heat
  4. Add minced garlic and cook until fragrant
  5. Drain the pasta, reserving a little pasta water
  6. Add the pasta to the pan and toss it with the gralic butter
  7. Add Parmesan cheese, black pepper, and a little pasta water if needed
  8. Garnish with parsley and serve immediately
